# Changelog: Bayanat Labs operating manual

All notable changes to the operating manual (`manual/stages.yaml` and the files generated from it). Versions follow semantic versioning: **patch** for wording, **minor** for tooling bumps and new optional checks, **major** for any gate or threshold change. Thresholds and stage definitions change only by pull request with founder approval. Every dataset card records the `manual_version` it was built under.

## [Unreleased]

- GPU calibration: GPU-hours, GPU type, hourly rate and cost per stage are still unmeasured; `manual/budget.py` keeps its GPU profiles as placeholders until a calibration run.
- Arabic edition: a human native review is recommended before wide promotion.
- Keep the score-block mirror and the `bl-score` pin in step with `bl-score` releases.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-15

First public version, approved by the founder on 15 Oct 2026.

### Contents
- `manual/stages.yaml`: the 13-stage gated pipeline (stage 0 scoping memo to stage 12 maintenance) with machine-checkable gate checks, per-stage thresholds, owners, sign-off roles, stop conditions and the mapping to the release gates G0-G7; `manual/schemas/stages.schema.json`. A stage, gate or stop rule that needs a legal position cites the decision ID of a recorded decision in the organisation's legal record (root `legal_record`); no stage waits for a separate legal sign-off.
- `manual/dod.yaml`: the 12-item definition of done in two phases (release candidate; done after launch).
- `manual/PUBLIC.md` and `manual/PUBLIC.ar.md`, "How Bayanat Labs builds open datasets", in English and Arabic, with `manual/stage_diagram.svg`. Their stage, threshold and definition-of-done sections are generated from `manual/stages.yaml` and `manual/dod.yaml`. The Arabic text has not yet been reviewed by a native Arabic writer; the English governs.
- `manual/gate-crosswalk.md`: stages × G0-G7, with twenty-one recorded choices (C-01 to C-21).
- `manual/SCOPING_MEMO.md`, the stage-0 template; `manual/scoping_memo.schema.json` for the rubric score block (a mirror of bl-score's memo schema); `manual/schemas/scoping_memo_record.schema.json` for the memo record. Each source records its licence route (`licence_route`) and release tier (`release_tier`), and a legal position is cited as `decision_ref`.
- `manual/schemas/reports/`: a JSON Schema for every report a check reads, generated by `scripts/gen_report_schemas.py`.
- `manual/etiquette.md` (crawler etiquette), `manual/stop_rules.md`, `manual/tooling.lock.yaml` (pinned tools and models with verified licences) and `manual/budget.py` (budget estimator: dollars only from a rate passed with `--rate`, clean hours only from a yield passed with `--yield`).
- `bl-score` is pinned by tree hash (`manual/bl-score.pin.json`, `scripts/pin_bl_score.py`, check `S0.score_tool_pinned`), not by its version string.
- `coordinator/`: ledger procedure, launch-bundle checklist, metrics digest row schema, maintenance calendar.
- `scripts/validate_stages.py` validates `stages.yaml` and `dod.yaml` and evaluates every gate against real artefacts; tests in `scripts/tests/` and `manual/tests/`.
