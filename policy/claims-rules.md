# Claims rules

> **Version 1.0.1 · Reviewed by counsel, October 2026.**
> Documentation, not legal advice.

Policy version 1.0.1 · Source: Bayanat Labs practice, adopted as policy · Licence: CC BY 4.0

Every "largest / first / only" claim is published only in the wording the claims ledger allows, with the counting rule, licence class, date and incumbent stated in the same sentence or footnote. The ledger lives in each repository's `CLAIMS.md` and in the site's claims register.

## Ledger rules

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| CL-1 | Count only what is released: hours after deduplication and filtering, words after normalisation, pairs after alignment filtering; manifests-only hours are always labelled "manifests". |
| CL-2 | Name the licence class in the claim ("openly licensed", "consented", "public-domain-verified"); never compare an open set with a non-commercial or request-only set without saying so. |
| CL-3 | Date every claim ("as of 18 Dec 2026") and keep the incumbent table in the card; re-check incumbents at each version. |
| CL-4 | "First" claims require a documented search of Hugging Face, Masader, Kaggle, Zenodo, the ACL Anthology and arXiv, saved in the evidence file. |
| CL-5 | Never claim "largest Arabic dataset" or "best" without a qualifier; never pool tiers; never derive a claim from a vendor's catalogue figure. |
| CL-6 | Figures about Bayanat Labs' commercial inventory stay in the commercial claims register and are never mixed with open-data counts. |

## Operating rules that apply the ledger

- Operating principle 4: "Claims go through the claims ledger before copy: every "largest" names the unit, licence class, date and the incumbent it beats."
- Every number is computed by a script from the released files, and every superlative is re-verified on release day.
- The founder approves every "largest / first / only" wording.
- The policy itself makes no "first" claim. Any "first public policy" wording needs the saved search in CL-4 first.
